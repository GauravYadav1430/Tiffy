About Tiffy

Tiffy was born out of a simple frustration: the hostel mess was boring, food delivery apps were expensive, and local aunties making incredible home food had no way to reach students. We built the bridge.

Our Mission

Connecting Students With Home-Cooked Food

Tiffy is a web-based platform designed for hostel residents at NIT Jalandhar. We solve a real problem: hundreds of local households prepare exceptional home-cooked tiffin services but have no digital presence, while thousands of students desperately want an alternative to the mess.

Unlike mainstream food delivery platforms that require certifications and charge high commissions, Tiffy is built specifically for this ecosystem — lightweight, accessible, and designed for both students with limited budgets and home cooks with limited tech experience.

The result: affordable, hygienic, home-style food for students, and a reliable income stream for local providers.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🏡

Home-Style Always

We believe the best food is made with care in someone's home kitchen. We never compromise on that.

💰

Affordable for Students

Hostel residents are on tight budgets. Our platform is designed to keep meal costs as low as possible.

🤝

Fair to Providers

We charge a minimal 5% platform fee — far less than mainstream apps — so providers keep most of what they earn.

🔒

Transparent & Secure

No hidden charges. Secure payments via compliant gateways. No raw financial data stored on our servers.

⚡

Simple to Use

Zero training needed for students. Minimal training for providers. The app should be as intuitive as WhatsApp.

🌿

Community First

We're building a local food ecosystem — connecting the campus community and strengthening local livelihoods.
